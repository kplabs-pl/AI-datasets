Morphological Leave-One-Out Kernel Density Estimates for Anomaly Detection in Satellite Telemetry

In this paper, we tackle the issue of the large number of false alarms raised by the existing anomaly detection methods and present Morphological Leave-One-Out Kernel Density Estimates (Mo-LOO-KDE) — an unsupervised anomaly detection method tailored for satellite telemetry data.
[M. Bikhit, S. Kandanaarachchi, K. Kotowski, J. Nalepa, B. Ruszczak: Morphological Leave-One-Out Kernel Density Estimates for Anomaly Detection in Satellite Telemetry, In: Machine Learning and Principles and Practice of Knowledge Discovery in Databases. International Workshops of ECML PKDD 2025, Springer, 2025, pp.104-121, DOI: 10.1007/978-3-032-19105-2_8]

  • DOI: 10.1007/978-3-032-19105-2_8

Single- and Multi-Objective Genetic Optimization of Hyperspectral Image Pre-Processing

In this paper we introduce genetic algorithms—both single- and multi-objective—for optimizing pre-processing in multi-target hyperspectral regression. Experiments on real-world hyperspectral data demonstrate that our approach significantly enhances the models for chlorophyll content estimation from hyperspectral images...
[ B. Ruszczak, S. Kubiciel-Lodzińska, K. Rudnik, et al.: Single- and Multi-Objective Genetic Optimization of Hyperspectral Image Pre-Processing, In: GECCO '25: Proceedings of the Genetic and Evolutionary Computation Conference, 2025, ACM, pp.899-902, DOI: 10.1145/3712255.3726774]

Datasets

Please find below the dataset we developed to support a reproducible research. We highlight here our recent collections, including hyperspectral images, spectroscopic measurements and space telemetry.

HyBEAR 🐻

It is a novel large-scale collection of high-resolution hyperspectral aerial images. It is the largest and most heterogeneous dataset for bare soil detection released to date.
Dataset link DOI

The dataset provides around 2000 hyperspectral images with manual labels for bare soil detection.
A dataset description and benchmark results may be found in our paper: DOI: 10.5194/essd-2026-64

TwinSpectra Dataset

It is a novel collection of two sets of spectroscopic measurements of refuse-derived fuel particles sampled during the cement manufacturing process.
Dataset link DOI

It contains 718 spectroscopic measurements with particle group labels and subset folds. The measurements were using two distinct devices. A dataset description and benchmark results may be found in our paper: DOI: 10.1016/j.dib.2026.112920

OPSSAT-AD benchmark

AI-ready benchmark dataset (OPSSAT-AD) containing the telemetries acquired on board OPS-SAT—a CubeSat mission, operated by the European Space Agency.
Dataset link DOI

It contains the telemetry data acquired on board a CubeSat mission that has been operated by the European Space Agency. It is accompanied by the paper with baseline results obtained using 30 supervised and unsupervised classic and deep machine learning algorithms for anomaly detection. A dataset description and the benchmark results may be found in our paper: DOI: 10.1038/s41597-025-05035-3

ESA Anomaly Dataset

It is the first large-scale, real-life satellite telemetry dataset with curated anomaly annotations originated from three ESA missions.
Dataset link DOI

It contains data from three ESA missions with curated anomaly annotations provided by experienced space operators.
